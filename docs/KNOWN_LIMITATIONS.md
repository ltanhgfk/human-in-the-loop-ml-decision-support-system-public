# Known Limitations

- The codebase targets legacy Java/web/SMS/MMS infrastructure.
- The original trained models and historical training data are not included.
- Exact historical deployment is therefore not reproducible from this repository alone.
- MMS/image handling exists in the original system design, but automatic image classification was future work rather than a demonstrated component of the thesis implementation.
- The original evaluation and historical data should be treated as research evidence, not as a modern benchmark.
